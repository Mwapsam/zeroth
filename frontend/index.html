    Terms & Conditions by Loan Type

    Expand any section to read the full terms. Clicking a service tile above jumps to the matching section.

    Savings Account — Terms
    • Minimum opening balance: ZMW 100. Minimum ongoing balance: ZMW 50.
    • Interest accrues daily and is paid on the last working day of each month at 10% p.a. (indicative — may vary).
    • Withdrawals above ZMW 20,000 require 48 hours' written notice to allow liquidity provisioning.
    • Accounts with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 consecutive months become dormant and attract a ZMW 15 monthly dormancy fee.
    • Reactivation requires valid ID and proof of residence.
    • ZBS is not a deposit-taking bank; savings are held with partner banks and covered under their deposit-protection schemes.
    Salary Advance — Terms
    • Eligibility: employed for 6+ months, salary paid into a bank account, age 21–60.
    • Maximum advance: 70% of monthly net salary, capped at ZMW 25,000.
    • Repayment: single deduction on next payday or in up to 3 instalments at 4% p.m. flat.
    • Latest payslip, employment letter and 3 months' bank statements are required.
    • Only one active Salary Advance per borrower at any time.
    • Borrower authorises employer payroll deduction and signs a deduction-authority form.
    • Late payment fee: ZMW 200 + 2% of overdue amount, plus credit-bureau reporting after 30 days.
    Vehicle Financing — Terms
    • Deposit: minimum 10% of vehicle price; maximum financed amount ZMW 500,000.
    • Interest: 5.8% p.a. fixed for the term; term 12–60 months.
    • Comprehensive insurance is mandatory for the full term; ZBS to be named as loss-payee.
    • The vehicle registration book / logbook is retained as security until final settlement.
    • Early settlement: allowed after 6 months, 2% early-settlement fee on the outstanding principal.
    • Vehicle may not be sold, exported or re-financed without written consent.
    • Missed payments: 30 days' grace; thereafter repossession proceedings may commence.
    Food Wallet Loans — Terms
    • Maximum per cycle: ZMW 2,000; limit replenishes after full settlement.
    • Purpose-locked: funds can only be used at approved grocery merchants.
    • Repayment term: 30 days from disbursement.
    • 0% interest when repaid on or before due date; 5% late fee thereafter, plus daily accrual.
    • One active Food Wallet Loan at a time.
    • Three consecutive late repayments suspend the wallet for 90 days.
    Group Loans — Terms
    • Minimum 5 members per group; each member must pass individual KYC.
    • Interest: 4% p.m. flat; term 6–24 months.
    • Joint and several liability: all members are liable for the whole loan, not just their share.
    • Group constitution and elected officials (chair, treasurer, secretary) required at application.
    • Loan decisions by group vote; disbursement to group bank account.
    • Failure by one member triggers a group-wide 30-day cure period before escalation.
    Emergency Loans — Terms
    • Same-day disbursement, subject to verification of the emergency.
    • Maximum: ZMW 5,000 per occurrence; one per calendar quarter.
    • Term: 14 days; flat fee 8% of principal.
    • Valid proof of emergency required (medical report, funeral programme, police report, etc.).
    • No rollover — must be settled before another Emergency Loan can be issued.
    • Non-payment reported to credit reference bureau after 45 days.
    Mortgage Loans — Terms
    • Interest: 4.2% p.a., variable — may be adjusted quarterly in line with the reference rate.
    • Term: 10–25 years; deposit minimum 20% of property value.
    • Property valuation by a ZBS-approved valuer; valuation fee borne by the borrower.
    • Life insurance and property insurance are compulsory for the full term.
    • Legal, stamp-duty and registration fees are payable by the borrower.
    • Property may not be sold, let or encumbered without ZBS's written consent until final settlement.
    • Early settlement: 3% early-settlement fee if within the first 5 years.
    • Default: legal action after 90 days; the property may be repossessed and sold.
    Investment Advisory — Terms
    • Minimum capital to open a managed portfolio: ZMW 1,000.
    • Target return: 6.1% p.a. — indicative only, not guaranteed.
    • 90-day lock-in period; early redemption attracts a 1.5% exit fee.
    • Quarterly statements; annual tax certificate provided.
    • Market risk disclaimer: capital is at risk, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
    • Advisory does not constitute a deposit or a bank guarantee.
    Credit Cards — Terms
    • APR on revolving balance: 7.5% (variable); interest-free period up to 25 days.
    • Minimum monthly payment: 5% of statement balance or ZMW 100, whichever is greater.
    • Annual fee: ZMW 500, debited to the card on each anniversary.
    • Foreign transaction fee: 2.5%; cash-advance fee: 3.5% (min ZMW 40).
    • Late payment fee: ZMW 150 + 3% of overdue amount.
    • Card block applied after 60 days of non-payment; account reported to credit bureau.
    • Cardholder is liable for all transactions made before the card is reported lost/stolen.
